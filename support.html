How do I free storage immediately after compression?

For safety, iOS moves replaced originals into the Apple Photos Recently Deleted album for 30 days. Open Apple Photos → Collections → Utilities → Recently Deleted → Select → Delete All to reclaim that space right away.

How do I restore my Lifetime Pro purchase?

Open FrameFold → Ledger tab (or Paywall) and tap Restore Purchases. Ensure you are signed into the same Apple Account used for the original purchase.

Can I give FrameFold access to specific photos only?

Yes. Choose Select Photos when granting Photo Library permission, then use the Manage Selected Media button inside Studio or Live Photo Studio anytime to add or remove items.

Why are iCloud-only videos skipped?

Because FrameFold is 100% offline-first and avoids hidden cellular data charges, videos stored only in iCloud are skipped until downloaded locally in Apple Photos.
